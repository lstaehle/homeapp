# Briefing

This is a briefing document for a project to build a family app with google calendar integration that pushes reminders via telegram to my wife and me, and let us allow to create new family appointments in the google calendar via the telegram app.

# Context

* We are a family of four (two adults, two kids in age of 7 and 5). Currently, we have proton.me family calendar that gets feeded via the proton calendar app. However, sometimes we miss appointments of the others as we are not consulting the app or have not set reminders in the calendar app. In the future, we want to receive telegram reminders every morning at 6 am that outlines upcoming appointments that day, as well as every monday morning upcoming events and appointments in that week. 
* Additional, we want to have a persistent display on a tablet in our kitchen that shows daily upcoming events, as well as an outlook of upcoming events that week. 
* The family app shall have a todoist integration where we have our grocery list and track items that we need to restock. The family app shall show which items need to be resupplied, so that everyone is aware if he goes to the supermarket that there are open items on the grocery list.
